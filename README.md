A complete story in exactly six words, written by a small Python program to
`/output/entry.txt`:

> She packed one bag. He watched.

One scene, one turn: she leaves for good, and he only watches — no plea,
no chase, no stop. The whole life they shared, and why it ended, is left in
the gap between the two sentences.
